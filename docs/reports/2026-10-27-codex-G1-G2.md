# Codex class pages: Groups 1 and 2

The class info text each ported class shows in the codex (CODEX_HeroInfo_<Class>), as the pack ships it today. Role and difficulty and the strengths/weaknesses lists come from Gloomhaven digital; the mechanics paragraph is ours, written from all the class's card halves (tools/gh_codex_text.py). Icons are shown as [Name].

## Group 1 (released)

### Brute

*Mechanics paragraph: ours (from the cards)*

Role: Melee Damage/Tank  
Difficulty: Low

Nearly every top is a melee attack, and the big ones are big: [Attack] 6 and 8 single hits, [Attack] 6 on every adjacent enemy, and five sweeping area attacks (Sweeping Blow, Leaping Cleave, Brute Force, Skewer, Whirlwind). Six cards [Push] 1-3 and two [Pull] enemies into reach; stuns, immobilizes and disarms ride on the hits, and three cards kill a normal enemy outright (one needs it stunned). The bottoms are movement with teeth: a jumping move that attacks everything it passes, a charge whose attack equals the hexes moved in a straight line, and Balanced Measure, which turns hexes moved into attack and damage dealt into movement.

Between the hits he holds the line with round-long [Shield] 1-2 and [Retaliate] 1-2 on many bottoms, a persistent prevent-3 (Juggernaut) and a toggle shield against attacks (Warding Strength). Three loot abilities, a couple of self-heals, almost no elements (one Earth, one Air; Skewer eats Air for pierce). Play him by moving into the middle of a group and hitting several of them at once.
  
Strengths:  
• High health and defensive abilities make him more durable than some  
• Access to early Disarm and Stun  
• Great at pushing enemies about
  
Weaknesses:  
• Low mobility  
• Poor ranged attack options  
• Not the greatest conversationalist

### Scoundrel

*Mechanics paragraph: ours (from the cards)*

Role: Melee Damage  
Difficulty: Low

A melee duellist who reads positions. Her attacks grow when the target is beside one of her allies (Single Out, Flanking Strike, Backstab, Open Wound, Cull the Weak, Gruesome Advantage, Sinister Opportunity: +2 or +3 [Attack], or [Wound]) or, the other way round, when the target stands alone: a persistent +2 against enemies with no allies next to them (Single Out), a round of it (Duelist's Advance), and a straight kill of an isolated normal enemy that is next to a friend of hers (Visage of the Inevitable). Stealth is the second engine: three cards make her [Invisible], and Smoke Bomb's attacks get stronger while she is unseen.

[Poison] runs through the deck (Venom Shiv, Special Mixture, Crippling Poison's +2 against poisoned targets, Visage poisons every adjacent enemy, Watch It Burn adds poison and wound to every attack, Cull the Weak adds 2 against any afflicted target). Her moves are long (5-7) and five cards loot, most of them every adjacent coin. A handful of thrown attacks (range 3-4, two to four targets) and two trap tricks (disarm, spring a trap on an enemy) round it out; defence is thin: one retaliate, one damage prevention, two self-heals.
  
Strengths:  
• High mobility  
• Potential for high melee Damage  
• Can afflict enemies with Poison
  
Weaknesses:  
• Little in the way of effects that assist teammates  
• Few defensive abilities and low health  
• Will try and take ALL the gold

### Sunkeeper

*Mechanics paragraph: ours (from the cards)*

Role: Melee Tank/Support  
Difficulty: Low

Everything runs on [CreateLight]: thirteen cards infuse it and twelve consume it, and what Light buys is the class: +1 [Attack] with advantage on her big melee swings (Hammer Blow, Defensive Stance, Path of Glory), an extra card recovered for an ally, an extra heal target, a longer strengthen, and Beacon of Light keeps Light lit at the end of every turn. She commands allies as much as she fights: five cards let an ally [Move] 4 or [Attack] 4-8 on her turn (Tactical Order, Empowering Command, Mobilizing Axiom for all of them, Illuminate the Target, Inspiring Sanctity) and Unwavering Mandate hands an ally a whole extra turn.

Protection comes as auras and stances: round-long [Shield] and [Retaliate], a persistent retaliate 2 and a persistent shield 1 (at the cost of a move), damage prevention for everyone adjacent, attackers of her allies at disadvantage, and Divine Intervention takes her allies' damage onto herself. Eight heals (3-5; Lay on Hands heals an ally's whole missing health for 3 of hers; Engulfing Radiance heals the whole party), [Bless] and [Strengthen] for allies, three ways to give allies cards back from discard or lost piles, and stuns on four of her own attacks.
  
Strengths:  
• Array of buff, heal and recovery options  
• Competent melee combatant  
• Uses Light to enhance her abilities
  
Weaknesses:  
• Can struggle against groups of smaller enemies  
• Weaker with fewer allies  
• Rather self-righteous a lot of the time

### Sawbones

*Mechanics paragraph: ours (from the cards)*

Role: Melee Damage/Support  
Difficulty: Low

The healer with a saw. Fourteen heals, 3 to 8, several of them paired with curing every negative condition (Curative Mixture, Research the Cure, Bedside Manner; Master Physician makes every heal cure first). He hands allies cards: Medical Packs ([Heal] 4 or 8, self) through six cards, and persistent [Shield] 1 or immunity to every condition that sit in the ally's active area (Booster Shot, Research the Cure, Vaccine). Two round-long modes turn his melee attacks into heals, and Triage heals 2 once a turn on top of whatever else he does.

His attacks are few but nasty: 3-7 with [Wound], [Immobilize], [Poison], [Stun] or [Disarm] on almost all of them, and three outright kills: Do No Harm (then he is disarmed and immobilized), Euthanize (a normal enemy with two or more conditions), Grisly Trauma (an area, enemies missing 10 or more health). Immunity auras for the party, two cards that recover discards for allies, and Teamwork, which moves allies and recovers one card per ally beside him.
  
Strengths:  
• Particularly efficient healer  
• Has a number of decent melee attacks  
• Medical Packs can also increase Ally's card counts
  
Weaknesses:  
• Poor ranged attacks  
• Weaker in parties with fewer allies or lower max health  
• Doesn't have as great an understanding of medical cleanliness as most doctors

### Quartermaster

*Mechanics paragraph: ours (from the cards)*

Role: Melee Damage/Support  
Difficulty: Medium

Items are his resource. Ten cards refresh spent or consumed items, his own or the party's (Restock for everyone in range 2, Side Pouch all of an ally's, Bag of Holding the party's small items, Reinforced Steel every round's end, Refreshment on each long rest, Continual Supply by discarding a card), and Proficiency makes every attack +1 in an action where an item was used. Three loots (2, 1 and 3 coins) keep the gold coming.

The weapons are the attacks: axe and spear sweeps (Cleaving Axe, Impaling Spear), a bow at range 3-4, a ballista that pierces everything for a round, a boomerang, and one-shot scrolls with the biggest effects in the deck: [Attack] 5 at range 3, 2 damage to every enemy within 4 (and 2 to himself), a 10-hex blizzard that immobilizes, a kill, and Catastrophic Bomb, 7 damage to every adjacent normal enemy and 7 to himself. For the party: +1 [Attack] and [Shield] 1-2 auras, +1 target on allies' ranged attacks (Quiver of Arrows), heal 2 to adjacent allies at every turn's end (Fortified Position), and an ally's whole discard pile back (Refreshment).
  
Strengths:  
• Many item refreshing abilities  
• Moderate offensive and defensive capabilities  
• Jack of all trades
  
Weaknesses:  
• Master of none  
• Weaker when party lacks good equipment  
• Always packs too much luggage

## Group 2 (testing)

### Cragheart

*Mechanics paragraph: ours (from the cards)*

Role: Damage/Support  
Difficulty: Medium

Earth and stone. [CreateEarth] is infused by eleven cards and spent by eight for +1 to +4 [Attack], +3 [Move], range, push or [Immobilize]. Obstacles are his toolkit: he creates them (Avalanche, Rock Slide with 2 damage around each, Petrify turns a killed normal enemy into rock, Meteor drops a triple rock and attacks), destroys them (Rock Tunnel, Explosive Punch hits everything adjacent, Rocky End attacks for each obstacle destroyed, Clear the Way moves one and clears traps along his path) and pushes enemies into them for 2 extra damage (Brutal Momentum, Heaving Swing, Pulverize). Twelve of his attacks hit several targets, many of them 4-7 hex areas.

He is careless with friends: six cards deal 1-2 damage to every adjacent figure, allies included (Rumbling Advance, Massive Boulder, Crater, Unstable Upheaval, Blind Destruction, Rock Slide). Jumping moves (five cards; Rock Tunnel immobilizes what it passes), a persistent [Retaliate] 2 and a ranged retaliate 3, five heals including a start-of-turn heal 2 for himself or an ally, a bear trap, and a ranged option that Backup Ammunition (+1 target) and Nature's Lift (+2 range) make real.
  
Strengths:  
• Can place and interact with obstacles  
• Versatile with melee & ranged options  
• Lots of direct damage abilities to dodge Shield and Disadvantage
  
Weaknesses:  
• Doesn't quite excel in any one aspect  
• Some abilities require obstacles in specific locations to function  
• Hard-to-read facial expressions

### Plagueherald

*Mechanics paragraph: ours (from the cards)*

Role: Ranged Damage/Support  
Difficulty: Medium

A ranged caster whose attacks spread sickness. Fourteen of twenty-four attacks are ranged (3 is the usual range) and six are area attacks, most of them 7-hex clouds and one of 14, that [Poison], [Immobilize], [Stun] or [Muddle] everything in them. [Poison] is the engine: he poisons on attacks, at range 5 (three targets), every enemy at once (Airborne Toxin), every adjacent figure after each move (Epidemic), and then collects: Succumb to the Gift kills a poisoned normal enemy, Accelerated End and Mass Extinction damage every poisoned enemy, Willing Sacrifice and Virulent Strain deal 3, Convert the Flock turns poisoned allies into attack bonus, and Spread the Plague adds poison to every attack for a price.

[Curse] is the second line: five attacks curse, Baneful Hex deals 3 whenever a curse is drawn, Mass Extinction curses and wounds everyone. [CreateAir] (nine infuses) feeds Foul Wind's persistent +1 [Attack] and Storm of Wings' line attack; [CreateDark] on four cards. Pierce 2 on two attacks, +2 targets from Grim Bargain, a persistent fly, small heals for the party, and a thin defence of shield 1s and attacker disadvantage.
  
Strengths:  
• Can often target multiple enemies  
• Effective at range  
• Powerful debuffs utilizing Curse and Poison
  
Weaknesses:  
• High potential for friendly fire  
• Low effectiveness versus single large targets  
• Harrower mating rituals are... unpleasant

### Nightshroud

*Mechanics paragraph: ours (from the cards)*

Role: Melee Damage  
Difficulty: Medium

[CreateDark] is the whole class: thirteen cards infuse it, eighteen consume it, and Nightfall relights it at the end of every turn. Spending Dark buys [Invisible], instant kills (Spirit of the Night a normal enemy, Swallowed by Fear a normal or elite, Angel of Death every adjacent normal enemy and one more on the bottom), +2 [Move] and +2 [Attack] at once (Smoke Step), a heal 4, a second attack, a 7-hex area, a second target, and a teleport to any connected room (Concealed Dominance).

Invisibility is the other half: four cards make him [Invisible] (more with Dark), and six attacks pay out while he is unseen: +2 [Attack] (Silent Force, Grim Sustenance, Quiet Frenzy), [Stun] (Soulfire), poison + muddle + wound (Lurking Ruin), a bonus [Attack] 2 after each attack (Black Knives) and a persistent stun on attacks (Voice of the Night). All of it is melee (a few range-3/4 throws with [Curse], [Muddle] or [Poison]), on long moves and jumps (6 twice), with attackers at disadvantage as the defence. Two loots.
  
Strengths:  
• Powerful execution abilities  
• Can use Invisibility to avoid damage  
• Uses Dark to enhance his abilities
  
Weaknesses:  
• Poor ranged options  
• Doesn't assist allies much  
• Finds making friends difficult

### Berserker

*Mechanics paragraph: ours (from the cards)*

Role: Melee Damage  
Difficulty: High

Her health is her resource. Attacks scale with the health she has lost (Resolute Stand), with lost cards (Growing Rage, Final Fight's move), with being below half health (+2 [Attack] on Defiance of Death, +2 [Move] on Break the Chains, Seeing Red's persistent +1 and [Shield] 1); Glass Hammer attacks for her current health and drops her to 1, Blood Pact hits 6 and costs half her health, and Fatal Fury kills a normal enemy whose health is under half of what she has lost. Eight cards let her pay 1-2 damage to herself for +2 attack, +1 move, [Stun], pierce, extra targets (Reckless Offensive, Flurry of Axes, The Maw of Madness) or [Shield].

What keeps her alive is built in: prevent 3 only when the hit would kill (Defiance of Death), prevent 5 persistent (Immortality), overheal to 26 (Unbridled Power), +2 on every heal she receives (Cauterize), [Attack] 3 back whenever she is damaged (Vengeful Barrage), immunity to every condition (Careless Charge), an improved short rest, four retaliates, and heals that grow with her missing health (From the Brink). [CreateFire] (five infuses) is spent for +2 attack, an attack 3, a retaliate 2. [Wound] on four cards, an area attack with wound (Devil Horns), and The Maw of Madness heals her 2 for every enemy it hits.
  
Strengths:  
• One of the highest damage-dealing classes  
• Gets even stronger on lower health  
• Can lead the front line and draw enemy fire
  
Weaknesses:  
• Little in the way of abilities that help allies  
• Operating on low health can lead to premature demise  
• Has anger management issues
