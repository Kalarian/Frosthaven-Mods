## Fixes to the game's engine (plugin, frosthaven.mods.dll)
- **End-of-action bonus with a requirement crashed the game** (`GHBonusRequirements`). Elementalist, Volatile
  Consumption top: "once per action, if you consumed an element, damage 1 or heal 1". Frosthaven asks such a bonus
  for its requirement without saying whose bonus it is, and the rules thread crashed (error screen). No Frosthaven card
  has this combination. The fix supplies the bonus's own holder. Verified in a bench.

## Fixes to the port (cards and pack)
- **Eye of the Void top** (Voidwarden): now works, with an approximation for you to confirm (manual test 16).
- **Collateral Damage top** (Demolitionist): the merged destroy-and-attack aimed the attack at the rock's hex. It is
  now a destroy with the attack as its sub-ability, written the way Rubble top is. Verified.
- **Negative Energy bottom** (Summoner): its curse around the summons was missing the flag that every Frosthaven card
  with this shape has. Fixed; not yet verified, because it needs summons out (manual 31).
- **Beast Tyrant**: the bear companion's definition is now in the pack (without it the class file failed to load), and
  Gloomhaven's "Command" banner shows as a "Command:" text row (Frosthaven's card reader rejected 19 cards with it).
- Icons for the class-named active cards of Elementalist, Soothsinger, Mindthief, Tinkerer and Beast Tyrant (they
  would have drawn as white boxes).

## Test-harness fixes (the cards were fine)
- A commanded summon (the bear) is now driven, and the nearest enemy is brought beside it when it has no target.
  Fifteen Beast Tyrant halves went from failing to passing.
- The "add a card to your hand" picker (Tinkerer, Chimeric Formula bottom) is answered.
- A song is judged as the bonus it sets. A commanded enemy's "+0" range and strength are no longer misread.
- A party of one never starts a scenario: single-class runs now get a partner (Summoner + Tinkerer; the Drifter in
  the full deck).
