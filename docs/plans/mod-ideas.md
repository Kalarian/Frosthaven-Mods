# Potential mods (ideas list)

Ideas players have asked for that we don't have yet. Each one, if built, gets its own host-controlled switch like
every other mod. Sizes are first guesses, before any research in the game's code: **S** = a small patch, **M** = new UI
or several patches, **L** = a big system, **?** = needs research first.

Sources: an r/Gloomhaven thread asking for mod ideas (2026-10-27, export read 2026-10-31).

## Open ideas

### Turn order and timing

| Idea | Notes | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Choose the short rest order | Rests are resolved in order; Geminate's short rest perk (and locked classes') care about it. Fits the long rest order plan. | S, with the long rest plan |
| Short rest while the others are still picking cards | Today everyone has to lock in cards before a short rest can happen | M |
| Do "your turn" things late: at end of round or during card selection | Realising you needed a short rest, drinking a potion to infuse an element you forgot, a stamina potion. The OP replied "shouldn't be too tough" | M |
| Decline the heal of a long rest or regenerate | Drifter's Continuous Health (+2 to the next 6 heals) gets used up on a wasted heal at full health; battle goals too | S |

### Not doable for now

- Classes that exist in neither digital game (e.g. Crimson Scales, newer mercenary packs): they need new models and
  animations.
- Running Frosthaven on a Mac: not something a mod can do.
