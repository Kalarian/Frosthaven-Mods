**Class mechanics:**

- Augment sharing: the first ally each round to perform a melee attack ability gains all your active augments (Many as One bottom)
- Control enemy abilities ('Control one enemy within Range 4:' then the enemy performs the listed ability): Submissive Affliction bottom, Withering Claw bottom, Possession top, Brain Leech top, Silent Scream bottom (Attack -1, relative to the enemy's own attack), Dark Frenzy bottom (two enemies), Domination top (two adjacent enemies)
- Control the target of your attack: The Mind's Weakness top, Psychic Blade top, Phantasmal Killer augment
- Damage to controlled enemies via consume: Submissive Affliction bottom (Ice, damage 2), Dark Frenzy bottom (Ice, damage 1 each), Phantasmal Killer augment (Dark, damage 3 after each control ability)
- Full-turn takeover: a normal/elite enemy swaps sides for its turn this round, you control it (Hostile Takeover bottom)
- Grant summons abilities: Feedback Loop (augment Attack +0, top Move +0), Possession bottom (Move 2), Telepathic Command (Attack +0 + Invisible; Move 4)
- Bonuses per negative condition on the target: Submissive Affliction top (+1 Attack), Perverse Edge top (+2 Attack and XP 1 each), Psychic Projection augment (+1 Attack on melee), Rat Monstrosity trait (+1 if any)
- Designated hex: attack from a designated enemy hex with a follow-up on its neighbours if it killed (Cranium Overload top); attack as though you occupied a designated unoccupied hex (Psychic Projection bottom)
- Conditions on enemies moved through: Feedback Loop bottom (Muddle, only if the jump ends where it started), Mass Hysteria bottom (Poison)
- Kill-conditional follow-ups: Invisible self (Hidden in the Shadows top), Attack 2 on adjacent enemies (Cranium Overload top); money token if the attack does not kill (Pilfer top)
- Invisible if not moved this round, then no move abilities this round and lose Invisible at round end (Hidden in the Shadows bottom)
- Conditional self-Strengthen if you performed the attack (Brain Leech bottom)
- Round melee bonus: add Curse to all your melee attacks this round (Psychic Blade bottom); use slots adding Stun + Curse to the next four melee attacks (Corrupting Embrace bottom, 4 slots, lost)
- Summons: Rat Swarm, Rat Monstrosity, Rat King (attack X = half its current HP, rounded up)
- Elements: Ice and Dark only (infuse boxes and consumes); conditions used: Poison, Muddle, Immobilize, Stun, Disarm, Wound, Curse, Invisible, Strengthen; no Ward/Safeguard
