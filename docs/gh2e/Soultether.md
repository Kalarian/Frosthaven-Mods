**Class mechanics:**

- Grant abilities to summons (the summon performs the granted ability; '+0/+1/+2' are added to the summon's own Move/Attack): one summon (Mighty Bond top, Bonded Might top, Unending Dominance bottom (fixed Move 2), Biting Wind bottom, Unwavering Hand top, Intervening Apparitions top, Void Imbuement bottom, Living Mountain bottom (Teleport 3), Primal Infusion top, Divided Mind bottom, Otherworldly Rage top, Interplanar Mastery bottom), two summons (Ethereal Gust top, Primal Assault top), all summons (Tear the Fabric bottom); element consume upgrades a one-summon grant to two summons (Void Imbuement bottom, Divided Mind bottom) or adds riders/area to the granted attack (Primal Assault top, Intervening Apparitions top)
- Granting an ability to a non-summon ally: the heal target gets Push 1 + Immobilize with Air consumed (Divided Mind top); the Rift Spirit grants an ally Shield 1 at the end of its turn, controlled by you (Planar Protection bottom)
- Control an enemy: you make it perform Move 1 (Unwavering Hand bottom), Attack -1 after the Fey Subjugator attacks it (Imposed Will top)
- Summon protection: use slots on attacks targeting summons (disadvantage + damage 1 to attacker: Living Night bottom), negate lethal damage + Invisible + Stun (Intervening Apparitions bottom), negate the next damage to a summon this round (Burning Passion bottom), Ward and damage 1 to attacker when an un-Warded summon is attacked (Imposed Will bottom), all summons Shield 1 + Retaliate 1 for the round (Tear the Fabric bottom)
- Summon offence buffs: next three summon attacks +1 Attack, Pierce 1 (Void Imbuement top), summons ignore Retaliate (Shadow Dart bottom), element-gated persistent buffs: Fire +1 Attack, Air +1 Move and Jump, Dark Shield 1 (Primal Infusion bottom)
- Element strong-or-waning conditions on summons: Jellyfish +3 Move with Air (Drifting Stingers), Ember Sprite Retaliate aura with Fire (Volatile Flame), Void Eater Curse on kill with Dark (Negative Energy), Primal Infusion bottom
- Summon death / kill triggers: wolf death infuses Dark and makes all wolves Invisible (Living Night), slime splits once per card using a character token on the card (Oozing Manifestation), Imp kills give the money token directly (Deadly Mischief), Void Eater kills shuffle a Curse into the monster modifier deck (Negative Energy)
- Summon end-of-turn actions: Rift Spirit grants Shield 1 (Planar Protection), Rock Colossus Heal 1 self (Living Mountain), Prismatic Unicorn infuses Fire, Air or Dark (Horned Nightmare)
- Summons whose attack is a condition icon with no value: Thorn Shooter (Poison), Rift Spirit (Muddle)
- Rock Colossus: +1 to all heals targeting it (Living Mountain)
- Recover lost cards: any one (Unending Dominance top), one with a summon action played at once, its summons gaining Ward, Invisible, Strengthen (Otherworldly Rage bottom); next summon action's summons gain Invisible (Unending Dominance top, 1-slot persistent)
- X = number of your summons: Attack X, Range X+1 (Strength in Numbers bottom); +1 Heal on targets adjacent to two or more allies (Strength in Numbers top)
- Teleport as the main movement: Mighty Bond, Burning Tether, Drifting Stingers, Shaping the Void, Living Mountain, Oozing Manifestation, Negative Energy, Horned Nightmare (bottoms), granted Teleport (Living Mountain bottom, Interplanar Mastery bottom)
- Stand-alone condition abilities: Disarm (Planar Protection top), Curse (Volatile Flame top), Poison (Oozing Manifestation bottom), Strengthen on summons/allies (Forged Ferocity, Burning Passion, Negative Energy), Ward (Shaping the Void top), Invisible self or on a summon (Unending Dominance, Tear the Fabric, Otherworldly Rage)
- Push damage: each target suffers damage 2 after the push (Biting Wind bottom)
- Elements used: Fire, Air, Dark only; many cards infuse in a '!' box and consume in pills, often three-way consumes (Primal Assault top, Interplanar Mastery top)
- Conditions used: Wound, Poison, Curse, Stun, Muddle, Immobilize, Disarm, Invisible, Strengthen, Ward; Pierce; disadvantage (Living Night bottom). No Bless, Safeguard, Regenerate.
