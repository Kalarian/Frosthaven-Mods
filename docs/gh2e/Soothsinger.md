**Class mechanics:**

- Songs: a top half with a 'SONG:' header (lute icon) followed by a cost in Notes; all 15 are persistent tops with a use-slot track (dashed first slot, XP 1 on slot 1, some also on a later slot) that ends after the last slot and is not lost. Costs: 1 Note: Mobilizing Measure (L), Bewildering Instrumental (H), Soothing Lullaby (H), Wistful Wounding (L); 2 Notes: Wearying Chant (H L), Defensive Ditty (H H), Echoing Aria (L L), Ode to the Gambler (H L), Alluring Serenade (L L), Disorienting Dirge (H H), Toughening Trill (H L); 3 Notes: Power Ballad (H H L), Dissonant Rhythm (H L L), Thrilling Solo (H L L); 4 Notes: Captivating Performance (H H L L)
- Song kinds: auras while active (Power Ballad +1 Attack on first attack each turn, Wistful Wounding Wound on first attack each turn, Defensive Ditty Shield 1, Echoing Aria Retaliate 1 range 2, Toughening Trill Shield 1 + Retaliate 1 range 2, all for you and all allies; Ode to the Gambler optional rolling first modifier; Disorienting Dirge disadvantage on all enemy attacks; Thrilling Solo repeat first attack ability each round with +1 Attack) and end-of-each-of-your-turns triggers (Mobilizing Measure, Bewildering Instrumental, Wearying Chant, Dissonant Rhythm, Soothing Lullaby, Alluring Serenade, Captivating Performance)
- Song track timing: the character token on an active song moves along the slots once per round (Grand Finale bottom: 'Skip moving your character token on active Song this round'; GHS perk text: 'Skip moving your character tokens on active songs at the end of this round'), so the slot count is the song's length in rounds. Captivating Performance bottom moves every active song's token back one slot
- Notes: two token kinds, High Note (treble clef in a ring) and Low Note (bass clef in a ring); GHS character tokens 'high-note' and 'low-note'. A large single clef on a non-song half gains that Note. Halves that gain a High Note: Dissonant Rhythm bottom, Increase Cadence top, Marching Beat top, Tuning the Outcome bottom, Singing Arrow top, Echoing Aria bottom, Onward to Battle top, Hymn of the Oak bottom, Alluring Serenade bottom, Change Tempo top, Pull the Strings top, Wistful Wounding bottom, Commanding Presence top. Low Note: Wearying Chant bottom, Intensifying Amplitude top, Marching Beat bottom, Tuning the Outcome top, Call to Action top, Singing Arrow bottom, Provoke Terror top, Soothing Lullaby bottom, Symphonic Medley top, Hymn of the Oak top, Change Tempo bottom, Thrilling Solo bottom, Inspiring Anthem top
- Notes of your choice: Increase Cadence bottom (one extra Note at the end of this and your next five turns, 6 slots, lost), Grand Finale top (one extra Note this round)
- Ignoring Note requirements: Symphonic Medley bottom (play up to three more cards from hand for one 1-, one 2- and one 3-Note song action), Grand Finale bottom (any number of cards from hand and discard pile for their song actions); both are lost and carry the card-stack-with-red-X ('unrecoverable') icon
- Granting abilities to allies ('Grant one ally ...:'): Mobilizing Measure (top: closest ally Move 2 each turn; bottom: adjacent ally Retaliate 1), Bewildering Instrumental bottom (adjacent ally Shield 1), Marching Beat bottom, Call to Action top, Singing Arrow bottom, Onward to Battle top, Hymn of the Oak bottom (all allies Shield 1), Inspiring Anthem top and bottom (no range), Wistful Wounding bottom, Commanding Presence top (Attack 3 on all enemies adjacent to the ally); most say 'within Range 3' with an enhanceable range
- Controlling enemies: Pull the Strings top (attacked enemy Move 2) and bottom (one enemy within range 5 attacks +0), Grand Finale top (every enemy moved through attacks 3)
- Character token on a monster: Soothing Lullaby bottom (Disarm at the end of that enemy's turn this round)
- Non-song use-slot cards: Increase Cadence bottom (6 slots, one per turn end), Intensifying Amplitude bottom (5 slots, one per ally attack, +X Attack where X = slots passed)
- Damage negation for an ally: Disorienting Dirge bottom (next damage an ally within range 2 would suffer this round)
- Positive conditions given at range: Strengthen (Marching Beat top, Inspiring Anthem top via an ally), Bless (Tuning the Outcome top, Change Tempo bottom), Ward (Hymn of the Oak top); negative: Curse, Muddle, Disarm, Stun, Wound, Immobilize. No summons, no elements on any card
- From GHS labels (not on the cards, for context): perks and masteries refer to Notes and songs, e.g. 'Whenever you perform a Song, if you have any unused Notes, gain one Note of your choice', mastery 'create 20 or more Notes and use each Note you create in a Song'

**Unclear:**

- Song rules beyond what the cards show: whether a song's top can be played without paying its Notes (Symphonic Medley and Grand Finale imply song actions normally require them), whether unused Notes carry over between rounds or have a cap, and whether the token advance happens at the end of the round (GHS perk text says so). The class rules aren't on the cards and no 2e rulebook or class mat is on disk
- Symphonic Medley bottom, Grand Finale bottom: the card-stack-with-red-X corner icon is read as 'unrecoverable' (as the Spellweaver/Doomstalker sheets read it, GHS flag 'loss'); the exact 2e rule wasn't checked
- Soothing Lullaby top: 'Heal 1, target all' with no range: whether it covers you and every ally on the map is a rules question
- Pull the Strings bottom: 'Attack +0' under a control is read as the enemy's own base attack +0; check the 2e control rule
