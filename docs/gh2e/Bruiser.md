**29 cards.** GHS agrees on 21 of 29; Xipe agrees on 13 of 28. Special rules to translate: 24.

**Class mechanics:**

- Round bonus 'gain XP 1 the next time you retaliate this round' (Eye for an Eye top, Provoking Roar top, Selfish Retribution bottom)
- X values from turn history: hexes moved this turn (Balanced Measure top), damage dealt this turn (Balanced Measure bottom, Frenzied Onslaught bottom), hexes moved by a straight-line move (Hook and Chain bottom)
- Linked move+attack that targets all enemies moved through, XP per target (Trample bottom); XP per enemy targeted (Whirlwind top)
- Use-slot persistent cards counting sources of damage: Shield 1 + Retaliate 1 per attack (Warding Strength bottom, 6 slots, lost), negate damage (Juggernaut bottom, 4 slots, lost); +1 Move on next two move abilities (Intimidating Growl bottom, 2 slots, persistent but not lost)
- Attack redirect: take attacks aimed at adjacent allies, ignoring range and line of sight (Fearsome Taunt bottom)
- Bonus on undamaged targets (Sweeping Blow top, Run Through top)
- Conditional attack bonuses: +2 Attack and XP 1 if you moved this turn (Unstoppable Charge top), +3 Attack and XP 1 if the target has fewer current HP than you (Face Your End bottom)
- Next-attack-ability buff this round (Push Through bottom)
- Triggered persistent: first Shield/Retaliate gained each round grants Shield 1 + Retaliate 1 for the round (Defensive Tactics top)
- Treat a move ability as an attack of equal value once per turn (Immovable Phalanx bottom)
- Push collision damage: enemies in hexes the pushed target enters suffer damage 4 + Wound (Let Fly top)
- Reaction after each attack targeting you this round: Move 1, then Push 2 on an adjacent ally or enemy (Brute Force top)
- +1 to all your Push and Pull, plus damage 1 to the moved enemy and its adjacent enemies after forced movement (Brute Force bottom)
- No summons; no new conditions (Muddle, Disarm, Stun, Wound, Immobilize only); no Ward/Safeguard

**Unclear:**

- Use-slot tracks (Warding Strength, Intimidating Growl, Juggernaut): the small red slash marks after the last slot appear on lost and non-lost cards alike, so I read them as 'end of track' and took the card's fate from the corner icons (Intimidating Growl: persistent only, so discarded, not lost)
- Sweeping Blow / Run Through: what 'undamaged target' means (full hit points, or not yet damaged this turn) is a rules question, not a reading one; Xipe uses full hit points
