- XP per enemy targeted (Disorienting Flash top, Ink Bomb top, Net Shooter top, Noxious Vials bottom); XP per enemy killed (Disintegration Beam top)
- Execute: kill all enemies in an area with current hit points <= 5 (Disintegration Beam top)

- Control an enemy: it attacks all adjacent enemies with its attack +2, then gains Stun (Harsh Stimulants bottom)
- Conditions applied to adjacent-to-target enemies: Muddle (Proximity Mine top)
- Round attack buff for you and adjacent allies (Enhancement Field bottom)
- Stand-alone condition abilities (no attack): Stun (Stun Shot top, Disorienting Flash top with Target 3), Immobilize (Net Shooter bottom), Strengthen (Volatile Concoction top, Pernicious Fogger bottom), Ward and Poison (Auto Turret bottom)
