**Class mechanics:**

- Light is the class element: infused by many halves (in '!' boxes) and consumed for bonuses; the usual attack consume is '+1 Attack, advantage, XP 1' (Hammer Blow top, Holy Strike top, Burning Flash top, Path of Glory top). One Dark consume that infuses Light (Daybreak top)
- Grant abilities: an ally performs the ability (Empowering Command bottom, Tactical Order top, Mobilizing Axiom bottom incl. self, Arresting March top + bottom, Divine Intervention bottom incl. self, Illuminate the Target bottom, Supportive Chant top); a follow-up heal targets 'the target of the grant ability' (Arresting March top)
- Recovering cards for allies: lost pile (Brilliant Prayer top, Bright Aegis bottom; both 'unrecoverable' lost), discard pile (Soothing Light top consume: level 1 card; Solar Bastion top consume: any card)
- Ally plays up to two cards immediately, one top and one bottom action (Bright Aegis bottom consume)
- Damage negation and redirect: negate all damage to you and adjacent allies this round (Protective Blessing bottom); persistent: take an ally's damage instead (Divine Intervention top)
- Disadvantage on enemy attacks: on attacks targeting you this round (Daybreak bottom), on attacks targeting you or allies within range 4 (Engulfing Radiance bottom)
- Attack modifier rewrites: enemies' positive and 2x cards count as +0 this round (Divine Intervention bottom consume); your null cards count as 2x when you consume Light during an attack ability (Scales of Justice bottom)
- Use-slot persistent buffs: Retaliate 3 vs adjacent attackers (Purifying Aura top, 5 slots, lost), +1/+2 Attack on allies' attacks, more if they have Ward (Sanctify top, 3 slots, not lost), heals splash to allies within range 2 of the primary target (Beacon of Light top, 3 slots, lost), Flying + +4 Attack and advantage on melee attack abilities (Divine Ascension top, 5 slots, lost)
- Persistent without slots: Shield 1 + Pierce reduction 2 + mandatory -1 Move (Defensive Stance bottom), Retaliate 2 (Scales of Justice top)
- Round-wide ally attack buffs: all allies +1 Attack, plus advantage with Light (Supportive Chant bottom)
- Advantage as an ability rider (attack consumes above, Supportive Chant bottom)
- Designated hex: Attack the enemy in a designated adjacent hex, Strengthen allies adjacent to you or that hex (Illuminate the Target top)
- Targets 'all allies moved through with the move ability' for Strengthen + Bless (Path of Glory bottom)
- Heal X = target's maximum hit points (Lay On Hands top)
- XP per enemy targeted (Burning Flash top)
- Conditions: Bless and Strengthen on self/allies (many cards), Ward (Sanctify top text, Beacon of Light bottom, Warding Blow top, Supportive Chant top), Stun, Muddle; no Safeguard, no summons

**Unclear:**

- Supportive Chant top: read as both rows (Ward, Strengthen, Bless 'self' and Attack 5) being part of 'Grant all allies within Range 3', with 'self' meaning each granted ally; the layout supports this but the 'self' pill could also be read as the Sunkeeper gaining the three conditions. Bottom: whether 'All allies' has a range or includes the Sunkeeper is not printed
- Engulfing Radiance top: Heal 5 'all' has no range printed (all allies anywhere, or a rules default?) and it is not stated whether the Sunkeeper is included
- Brilliant Prayer top / Bright Aegis bottom: the card-stack-with-red-X corner icon is read as 'unrecoverable' following the other 2e sheets; the exact 2e rule was not checked
- Illuminate the Target top: the header wording 'Designate an adjacent hex containing an enemy to perform:' is transcribed as printed; whether the attack is still a normal melee attack from you (it reads so) is a rules question
- Divine Ascension top: the wing icon is read as Flying; how long Flying lasts (while the card is active, i.e. until the fifth slot) is implied, not printed
- Empowering Command top: Strengthen, Bless with a Range 3 pill and no target text; read as one target (an ally) within range 3, with the diamond+ mark after Bless covering the pair
