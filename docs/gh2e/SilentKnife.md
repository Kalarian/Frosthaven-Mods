**Class mechanics:**

- Isolated-target bonus, 'adjacent to none of its allies' (Single Out top and bottom, Sinister Opportunity top, Hidden Daggers bottom, Gruesome Advantage top, Serrated Arrow bottom, Stiletto Storm top, Visage of the Inevitable top, Crippling Poison bottom)
- Flanking bonus, 'adjacent to any of your allies' (Flanking Strike top, Sinister Opportunity top, Backstab top, Open Wound top, Gruesome Advantage top, Serrated Arrow bottom, Visage of the Inevitable top); both at once: advantage + XP (Sinister Opportunity), double attack (Serrated Arrow), Disarm + Invisible + XP (Visage)
- Double the value of the next attack (Smoke Bomb top while Invisible, Serrated Arrow bottom)
- Use-slot persistent next-attack buffs: Single Out bottom (4 slots, lost), Stick to the Shadows top (3 slots, not lost), Smoke Bomb top (1 slot, lost), Trickster's Reversal top (1 slot, not lost), Serrated Arrow bottom (1 slot, lost)
- Attack bonus from the target's Shield value: +(X+2) on the next melee attack against a shielded enemy (Trickster's Reversal top)
- Negate damage: next damage this round (Trickster's Reversal bottom); damage and added effects of all attacks this round, plus XP 1 and an Attack 2 counterattack on each attacker (Dance of Daggers top)
- Advantage: conditional (Sinister Opportunity top, Cull the Weak top), all attacks this round (Dance of Daggers bottom)
- Target restrictions: normal monster bonus (Cull the Weak top), only enemies with current HP 2 or less (Cull the Weak bottom)
- Invisible on self: Smoke Bomb top, Stick to the Shadows bottom (only if adjacent to no enemies, lost at end of round), Visage of the Inevitable top (conditional)
- Money tokens: loot every hex moved through (Swift Bow bottom); place one of your money tokens in the target's hex for +2 Attack, Pierce 2 (Flintlock top); trap in the hex of a looted money token (Flintlock bottom); spend a money token, converted enemy drops an extra one (Hired Help top)
- Traps: optionally don't spring traps while moving, destroy a trap for XP 1 (Practiced Reflexes bottom); create a Poison+Immobilize trap (Flintlock bottom); designate, relocate and spring a trap on an enemy (Spring the Trap bottom)
- Control an enemy: one Move 1 ending adjacent to you (Smoke Bomb bottom); permanent conversion of an adjacent normal enemy into an ally (Hired Help top)
- Conditional follow-up abilities: second attack if all targets were isolated (Stiletto Storm top); Move 3 Jump + XP if at least three enemies were attacked (Watch it Burn top); Attack 2 Range 3 Poison after the next melee attack on a poisoned enemy this round (Gruesome Advantage bottom)
- Persistent global attack riders: Poison on all attacks, +1 Attack vs poisoned enemies (Crippling Poison top); round riders: Pierce 2 on ranged attacks (Venom Shiv bottom), Immobilize vs isolated enemies (Crippling Poison bottom)
- Action usable while stunned + remove all negative conditions (Spring the Trap top)
- Play a card from hand during your turn as an extra action (Watch it Burn bottom)
- Elements: infuse Dark (Special Mixture top, Smoke Bomb top), consume Dark (Backstab top), infuse Fire (Flintlock top, Watch it Burn top)
- No summons; conditions used: Poison, Wound, Immobilize, Disarm, Muddle, Invisible, Stun (only as 'while you have Stun'); no Ward/Safeguard
