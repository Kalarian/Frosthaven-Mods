**Class mechanics:**

- Medical Packs: six level-'M' ability cards (initiative 50, numbers 0440-0445), all top Heal 4, self, each with a different bottom: Heal 2 self + Ward (0441), Heal 3 self + Safeguard (0442), Heal 3 self + Bless (0443), Heal 1 self + Strengthen + mandatory self-Poison (0444), remove one negative condition you have + Heal 3 self (0445), Heal 6 self + mandatory self-Immobilize (0440). The Sawbones gives them to allies: 'Give one adjacent ally one medical pack to gain XP 1' (Battlefield Medicine top, Hand of the Surgeon bottom, Surgeon's Satchel bottom), 'give them one medical pack' to the Safeguard target (Blood Transfusion bottom), 'gain one medical pack' yourself at the start of your next three turns (Take Your Medicine top). Allies hold them in hand as ability cards: Take Your Medicine bottom lets each ally immediately play one from hand for one action; Secret of the Oak bottom triggers on 'a heal ability on a Medical Pack ability card'. How a pack is normally played, which pack is given, and what happens to it after use are not on the cards.
- Prescriptions: halves with a 'Prescription' tab (scroll icon) are persistent use-slot effects, XP 1, not lost: Booster Shot top (Shield 1 vs next four attack damage sources), Syringe top (+1 Attack on next four attacks), Vaccine bottom (prevent next three enemy-applied negative conditions), Regenerative Tissue bottom (Heal 2 self at start of next three turns below max HP), Fortifying Injection top (Ward self at start of next four turns without Ward), Exacting Anger bottom (Strengthen self at start of next three turns), Take Your Medicine top (gain one medical pack at start of next three turns). Other cards refer to allies having active Prescriptions: Bloody Saw top (+1 Attack, Wound, XP 1 if an ally has one), Experimental Stimulants top (XP 1 + negate next damage if the ally has one), Return the Favor top (grant Move 3 + Attack 3 to an ally who has one), Secret of the Oak top (XP 1 + Shield 2 if the heal target has one), Precaution top (each ally may move the token back one slot on one active Prescription), Prevention is Key bottom (trigger on your next Prescription action). So Prescriptions can be active on allies (the 'you' on the card then presumably being the holder), but how they get there is not on the cards.
- Bonuses against damaged targets: Vital Strikes top (+1 Attack, persistent), Vaccine top (+1 Target, XP 1), Prep for Surgery top (Stun, XP 1), Euthanize top (execute)
- Execute: kill an adjacent damaged enemy with at least two negative conditions and 8 or fewer hit points (Euthanize top)
- Granting abilities to allies: Teamwork (Attack 3 / Move 3), Experimental Stimulants top (Retaliate 2), Return the Favor top (Move 3 + Attack 3), Precaution top (Shield 1 to all allies and self), Prevention is Key bottom (Shield 1 to adjacent allies and self), Secret of the Oak top (Shield 2)
- Negative condition removal: Curative Mixture (one, before a heal / on an adjacent ally), Blood Transfusion bottom (all), Secret of the Oak top (all, before the heal), Return the Favor bottom (one, may transfer it to an adjacent enemy), Medical Pack 0445 (one of your own)
- Damage and condition negation: Experimental Stimulants top (next damage this round), Fortifying Injection bottom (all damage to adjacent allies this round), Vaccine bottom (prevent three negative conditions)
- Treat attack and move abilities from ability cards as heals of value +1, ignoring added effects; converted moves heal at range 1 (Prep for Surgery bottom)
- Heal X = damage enemies suffered from the card's attack ability (Blood Transfusion top)
- Heal that may target an enemy, then mandatory Stun on the heal target (Amputate bottom)
- Character token on an enemy: re-Stun it the next three times it loses Stun (Do No Harm top, use slots, lost)
- On-kill effect: Wound all enemies adjacent to the killed target's hex (Hack to Bits top)
- Next-ability buffs this round: +1 Target on next melee attack ability (Grisly Trauma bottom); +4 Attack or +4 Move after a heal from a Medical Pack card (Secret of the Oak bottom, persistent)
- Start-of-turn use-slot triggers, some conditional on your state (Regenerative Tissue, Fortifying Injection, Exacting Anger, Take Your Medicine)
- Mandatory self-negative conditions in '!' boxes: Hamstring bottom (Immobilize), Do No Harm top (Disarm), Medical Pack 0444 (Poison), Medical Pack 0440 (Immobilize); Amputate bottom's Stun on the heal target is also in a '!' box
- Conditions used: Wound, Poison, Stun, Immobilize, Disarm, Muddle, Strengthen, Ward, Safeguard, Bless (Medical Pack 0443). Safeguard is a 2e condition Frosthaven may lack. No summons, no elements.

**Unclear:**

- Medical Packs: the rules for using them (how an ally plays one, whether it is in addition to their two cards, which of the six is given, and whether it returns to the Sawbones afterwards) are not printed on any card; check the 2e class rules / rulebook
- Prescriptions: the cards never say how a Prescription becomes active on an ally, while several cards assume allies can have one (Bloody Saw, Experimental Stimulants, Return the Favor, Secret of the Oak, Precaution); check the 2e class rules
- Medical Pack 0443 bottom: read the gold icon as Bless (Frosthaven-style Bless icon, zoomed); fairly but not fully certain
- Prevention is Key top: one diamond mark printed after the pair 'Wound, Disarm'; which ability it enhances is not shown (GHS puts it on Disarm)
- 'Damaged' (Vital Strikes, Vaccine, Prep for Surgery, Euthanize) is a rules question (below maximum hit points, presumably), not a reading one
- Do No Harm top: the slot track starts with a dashed slot; how that interacts with 'the next three times they lose Stun' (does the attack's own Stun use the first slot?) is a rules question
- Amputate bottom: GHS's target fields are unresolved keys, so GHS's exact target wording could not be compared; the card reads '1 ally, enemy, or self' and 'the target of the heal ability'
