**Class mechanics:**

- Bear companion: permanent summon from the start of each scenario (stats in summons); acts before you; you may lose cards to negate its damage; dead = gone for the scenario
- COMMAND halves (banner; the Bear performs the whole half): Maul top+bottom, Howling Bolts bottom, Gnaw top+bottom, Disorienting Roar top, Spirit Swap top, Invocation of Endurance bottom, Forceful Swipe top+bottom, Energizing Strike top, Punch Through top+bottom, Unstoppable Beast top+bottom, Rampage top+bottom, Blood Hunger top+bottom (bottom is a persistent Shield 1 + Retaliate 2 on the Bear), Unleashed Fury top+bottom, Lash Out top, Jaws of Death top+bottom
- Invocation use-slot triggers 'after each of the Bear's next N attacks, triggering no more than once each round' (persistent, not lost, XP 1 on the dashed first slot): command Move 3 + Jump (Disorienting Roar bottom), command Heal 2 self (Invocation of Endurance top), Wildfury Attack 2 from the Bear's hex (Invocation of Spirit top), control the attack's target: Move 2, Attack 2 (Invocation of Domination top, 4 slots), damage 1 to all enemies within range 2 of the Bear + Bear Heal X self (Invocation of Absorption top)
- Required-element halves ('!' consume box at the half's top-left, then a colon; mat: cannot perform without the element): Disorienting Roar bottom (Air), Invocation of Endurance top (Earth), Invocation of Spirit top (Air|Earth), Gust Sigil top (Air), Stone Sigil top (Earth), Invocation of Domination top (Air), Invocation of Absorption top (Earth), Spirit Sigil top (Air and Earth)
- Split Air|Earth element icons (one pill, either element): consume (Spirit Swap top, Invocation of Spirit top, Ancient Ward bottom), infuse (Unbreakable Bond top, Spirit Sigil bottom); both elements as separate circles: consume (Ancient Ward top, Spirit Sigil top), infuse (Forest's Blessing bottom)
- Persistent damage-token counter on the card, cashed in for +3 Attack on the Bear's attack (Concentrated Rage bottom)
- Perform an attack as if you occupied the Bear's hex (Invocation of Spirit top, Unbreakable Bond top)
- Bear-conditional effects: extra grant if the grant target is the Bear (Focused Aggression bottom), +1 Heal if the target is the Bear (Invocation of Spirit bottom), attack only if adjacent to the Bear (Stone Sigil bottom)
- Grant abilities to an ally: Shield 1 to an adjacent ally (+ Retaliate 1 if the Bear) (Focused Aggression bottom); Shield 1 to an ally within range 4, then Poison all enemies adjacent to that ally (Invocation of Domination bottom)
- Position swaps by simultaneous teleport: two normal/elite enemies (Spirit Swap bottom), Wildfury and Bear (Lash Out bottom); teleport the Wildfury to an adjacent empty hex during the Bear's action (Spirit Swap top)
- Aura summons with no move/attack (persistent, not lost): Wind Idol (Pierce 1, Gust Sigil), Monolith (Shield 1, Stone Sigil), Vital Force (+2 Attack on first attack each round, Spirit Sigil)
- The Bear loots, the Wildfury gains the loot (Gnaw top)
- Control an enemy (Invocation of Domination top)
- 'If you performed the attack ability, perform:' follow-ups (Energizing Strike top: Strengthen self; Blood Hunger top: Heal 3 self)
- Round debuff: Muddled enemies within range 2 get -1 Attack this round (Disorienting Roar top)
- Double all Attack bonuses from ability cards on one attack (Unleashed Fury top)
- Kill-repeat: if the attack kills, gain XP 1 and repeat Move 4 + Attack 6 (Jaws of Death top)
- Self-losing persistent: lost at the end of a round in which 'you' neither attacked nor were attacked (Blood Hunger bottom)
- Skip your card selection and turns for three rounds; the Bear takes two turns at initiative 14 each round, your other persistents do not advance (Unbreakable Bond bottom)
- Conditions used: Disarm, Poison, Muddle, Wound, Immobilize, Strengthen, Bless, Safeguard (Frosthaven-era: Nature's Gift top, Ancient Ward bottom, Spirit Sigil bottom); no Ward

**Unclear:**

- Command halves that say 'you'/'your': Punch Through bottom ('all your attacks this round'), Energizing Strike top and Blood Hunger top ('if you performed the attack ability', 'self'), Blood Hunger bottom ('if you neither attacked nor were attacked'), Unleashed Fury top ('your ability cards'): read as the Bear, but whether 'you' means the Bear or the Wildfury is a rules question, not a print one
- Spirit Swap top: 'Teleport the Wildfury to an adjacent empty hex' does not say adjacent to whom (read: to the Bear, the performer of the Command half)
- Invocation tracks (Disorienting Roar, Invocation of Endurance/Spirit/Domination/Absorption) and Unbreakable Bond: the first slot is dashed although the text counts 'the next three/four attacks' (or 'the next three rounds'); whether the dashed slot is marked on play (README rule) and so leaves one trigger fewer is a rules question
- Energizing Strike bottom: 'Heal 1, target all allies' prints no range (whole map?) and whether it includes the Wildfury
- Disorienting Roar top: 'within Range 2' measured from the Bear (performer of the Command half) - read so, not stated
- Disarm: identified by elimination (grey diamond with an open hand); no legend on the class mat
