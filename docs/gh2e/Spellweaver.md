**27 cards.** GHS agrees on 21 of 27; Xipe agrees on 6 of 26. Special rules to translate: 24.

**Class mechanics:**

- Delayed trigger 'when you next perform an action with [lost] this round' (round bonus): infuse any (Aid from the Ether bottom), gain Ward (Etheric Echo bottom), recover one discarded card (Searing Glacier bottom), Attack 3 Range 3 after the action (Twin Beams bottom); persistent one-use version: +1 Attack to all attacks of the next attack ability from a lost action (Warm Up top)
- Play a card from a pile and perform its lost action: level 1 card from the lost pile, not Reviving Ether (Etheric Echo top); any card from the discard pile (Spell Mastery top)
- Recover all lost cards, the card itself unrecoverable (Reviving Ether top)
- Consume any element (wild) for bonuses: Arcane Bolt top, Aid from the Ether top, Cool Down top, Elemental Rays top, Cold Fire bottom, Frostflare Orbs bottom; Fire/Ice consumes throughout, two independent consumes on one ability (Cold Fire top, Chromatic Explosion bottom)
- Consume grants a whole separate ability (Dancing Gales top: Strengthen self; extra Attack 2 Range 3 Muddle)
- Consume grants a round effect: advantage on all attacks (Frost Strike bottom), +1 Attack on all attacks (Cold Fire bottom)
- Use-slot persistents: +2 Attack on the next four single-target ranged attack abilities (Flameswell top, 4 slots, lost); negate the next three sources of 2 or more damage, on an ally marked with a character token (Ice Armor top) or on yourself (Ice Armor bottom), 3 slots each, lost
- Buffs on allies: Shield 1 for the round (Emberfrost bottom); Strengthen + next attack +2 (Heatwave bottom); Shield 3 against the next attack targeting them, with consume Fire = damage 3 to the attacker, consume Ice = +3 Shield (Chromatic Explosion bottom); Heal 4 + Strengthen + Bless on allies in the attack's red hexes (Chromatic Explosion top)
- Special targeting: the 4 closest enemies within 5 hexes (Freezing Nova top); all enemies within 2 hexes (Inferno top); all enemies within range 4 of a designated hex, pulled toward it (Freezing Vortex bottom)
- Execute + terrain: kill targets at 2 or fewer HP after the attack and create 1-hex obstacles in their featureless hexes (Freezing Vortex top)
- Damage to allies: allies within range 2 suffer 2 (Inferno top); all allies and enemies in your room suffer 4 (Inferno bottom)
- Infuse all six elements at once (Chromatic Explosion top)
- Conditions used: Ward (Flameswell bottom, Etheric Echo bottom), Bless (Chromatic Explosion top), Curse + Stun as a non-attack ability (Elemental Rays bottom), Strengthen, Muddle, Immobilize, Poison, Wound, Stun; advantage (Frost Strike bottom)
- No summons

**Unclear:**

- Reviving Ether top: the corner icon 'card stack with a red X' beside the lost icon is read as 'unrecoverable' (GHS flag 'loss', Xipe 'Permanently Lost' agree); the exact 2e rule wording for it was not checked
- Class-wide: the small '!' tab on every element/corner box and on Inferno's 'All allies within Range 2 suffer damage 2' row; its rule meaning is not decided here
- Freezing Nova top / Inferno top: 'within 5 hexes' / 'within 2 hexes' (distance, possibly without line of sight) versus a Range value is a rules question; Xipe uses Range
- Warm Up top: a use track with a single dashed slot and no further slots; read as one use that starts on play
- Spell Mastery top: where the card played from the discard pile goes afterwards is not printed (GHS adds 'moved to your lost pile'); rules question
- Chromatic Explosion top: whether 'all allies in the red hexes' can include yourself is a rules question
